About us

Care your family can trust

Merit Health Centre was founded on a simple belief: Cambodian families deserve better than untrained, unsupervised care for the people they love.

Every Merit patient is cared for by professional registered nurses — many with international-hospital experience — working under documented care plans, structured shift handovers and real clinical supervision. Today, twelve families across Phnom Penh trust us with their loved ones' care.

Good to know

Common questions

Who exactly will care for my parent?

Registered nurses only — uniformed, carrying photo ID, trained on Merit protocols and supervised by our senior nurse. On 24-hour packages, two nurses cover each day in 12-hour shifts with a structured handover between them.

How does payment work?

Packages are monthly and billed in advance at the start of each month. The assessment visit is free, and we confirm your exact package before care begins. Add-on services are quoted separately.

Can you work with our own doctor?

Yes — we prefer it. Your care plan is built with your doctor's input, and our nurses report observations back so your doctor always has a clear picture.

How does the neurology referral service work?

After a clinical review, we match your case to the right specialist centre in our six-country network, prepare your records, arrange appointments, travel and interpreting, and plan your follow-up care at home when you return. Fees are explained transparently at assessment.

Which areas do you cover?

We currently serve families across Phnom Penh. Contact us to confirm coverage for your location.

Do you speak English?

Yes — our team works in Khmer and English, and we arrange interpreting for overseas treatment journeys.
